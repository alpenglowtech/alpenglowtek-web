ANIX

Ultra-Low-Power AI Inference Core, Opening the Frontier of Next-Generation AI Applications

Scroll

ANIX is an ultra-low-power AI inference core, built for always-on intelligence in harsh power-constrained environments, bringing AI into devices where intelligent compute was previously impossible to deploy.

Technology

Core Technology

Analog Compute-in-Memory Architecture

Compute units are fused with the memory array — multiply-accumulate (MAC) operations execute in place using analog circuitry, eliminating the data-movement overhead inherent to von Neumann architectures. Compared to digital implementations, analog compute achieves lower power within a smaller circuit area.

Always-On Sensing, Low-Power Operation

Supports always-on sensing and real-time inference within milliwatt (mW)-class power budgets, meeting the endurance demands of battery-powered endpoints.

Multi-Precision Programmable Compute

Supports multiple compute precisions including 4-bit and 8-bit, covering common operators such as convolution (Conv) and fully-connected (FC) layers — flexibly accommodating diverse neural network model deployments.

Consumer Electronics
